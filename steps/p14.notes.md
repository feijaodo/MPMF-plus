# P14 functional extraction pilot ("used for" / "part of"), research, store copy only

Scripts: steps/p14_extract.py (extract + write FUNCTIONAL-plane edges into a COPY), steps/p14_judge.py (40-sample precision).
Private outputs (chat content) stay outside the repo: /root/p14/out/{edges.jsonl,judged.json,extract_summary.json}.

Setup: same as p13 but new seed. 100 random conversations (seed 1414, 8..60 facts, 299 eligible of 820), 2277 facts.
Extractor deepseek-v4.1-flash temp 0, strict prompt; pair = (rel, fact a, fact b), USED_FOR = a is the means, b the
purpose; PART_OF = a is the part, b the whole. Edges: Plane.FUNCTIONAL, labels USED_FOR / PART_OF, a -> b.
Result: 104 edges in 30/100 conversations (39 USED_FOR, 65 PART_OF; 75 explicit, 29 implicit). 65/100 replies parsed;
35 came back empty after the retry ladder (counted as no edges; recall not measured).
Judging: 40 random edges (seed 1415); judge sees the two facts + source messages + title, not the extractor's basis.

Precision (primary judge claude-sonnet-5.5): 17/40 = 0.425 (Wilson 95% CI 0.29-0.58); 16 not_related, 3 wrong_relation,
4 unjudged (empty judge reply, counted as misses; 17/36 = 0.47 if dropped), 0 reversed.
Second judge gpt-4.1-mini: 24/40 = 0.60 (more lenient); both say correct 12/40 = 0.30; verdict agreement 0.45.
By relation: USED_FOR 4/11, PART_OF 13/29 (small n, no difference to claim). Explicit 16/30 = 0.53, implicit 1/10 = 0.10.

Good: "NOESIS Group (7 agents) -> the 15-agent Quorum Architecture"; "offending key at line 6 of known_hosts -> the known_hosts file";
"organise thoughts into an X post -> make the case for a company purchase"; "icing the lower back -> the combined recovery plan".
Bad: topic neighbours called parts ("wheels are 180 degrees symmetrical" -> "the car is made of wheels"); a goal or
criterion called a "means" (USED_FOR); a sibling item called part of its sibling; restatement of the same plan.

Verdict: ~0.43 precision, below the causal pilot (0.67) and too low to feed the live graph or typed_walk. PART_OF is
mostly a part/whole restatement inside one message; USED_FOR is confused with goal/criterion. The implicit cases are almost
all wrong, so only explicit edges plus a second-stage verifier could be worth another pilot. Nothing written to the live
store; the 104 edges exist only in /root/p14/athena_p14.mpmf.
